Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short

Pennylog has no server and does not collect your data. Your expenses stay on your phone and, if you sign in with Google, in a private folder of your own Google Drive. The people who run the app cannot read them.

Why the app asks for access to Google Drive

So that you do not lose your expenses if you change phone or clear your browser data, the app saves a copy in your Google Drive. This is how it works:

  • It asks for a single permission: “See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive” (drive.appdata).
  • This permission gives access to a hidden folder created just for the app. You will not see it among your Drive files and no other app can read it.
  • The app cannot see, open, change or delete your documents, photos or any other file in Drive.
  • The folder holds a single file with your expense notes. It does not go through any server of ours: it travels directly from your phone to Google.
  • If you sign in on another phone with the same Google account, your expenses are there.

Google user data

  • What the app accesses: the name and email address of the Google account you sign in with (scopes openid, email, profile), and the app’s own hidden data folder in your Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). Nothing else.
  • How it is used: the name and email are shown in Settings so you know which account is connected. The Drive folder stores one file with your expenses, only to give you backup and sync across your devices.
  • How it is stored: the name and email stay on your device. The expenses file stays in your Google Drive, under your control.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people.

Pennylog’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What data the app handles

  • Expenses, categories, notes and budgets that you enter. They are stored on your device (in the browser) and in your Google Drive.
  • The name and email of your Google account, read when you sign in, only to show which account you are connected with. They stay on your device.
  • Receipt photos: reading happens on your phone. Images are neither uploaded nor stored; only the amount you confirm is kept.
  • Preferences such as language and currency, stored on your device.

What we do not do

  • No server of ours receives your data.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no usage analytics.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • The only external service involved is Google (sign-in and Drive), under Google’s privacy policy. The first time you scan a receipt, the reading engine is downloaded from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr).
  • The website and the app files are served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

How to delete your data

  • From your phone: Settings → Sign out / switch account removes the data from the device.
  • From Drive: revoke the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps you can delete the app’s hidden data.
  • History: Settings → Clear history deletes closed periods and their expenses, on every device you use.
