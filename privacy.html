Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short

Pennylog has no server and does not collect your data. Your expenses stay on your phone and, if you sign in with Google, in a private folder of your own Google Drive. The people who run the app cannot read them. The website, unlike the app, uses Google Analytics for visit statistics, but only if you consent (see “Website, cookies and Google Analytics” below).

Why the app asks for access to Google Drive

So that you do not lose your expenses if you change phone or clear your browser data, the app saves a copy in your Google Drive. This is how it works:

  • It asks for a single permission: “See, create and delete its own configuration data in your Google Drive” (drive.appdata).
  • This permission gives access to a hidden folder created just for the app. You will not see it among your Drive files and no other app can read it.
  • The app cannot see, open, change or delete your documents, photos or any other file in Drive.
  • The folder holds a single file with your expense notes. It does not go through any server of ours: it travels directly from your phone to Google.
  • If you sign in on another phone with the same Google account, your expenses are there.

Google user data

  • What the app accesses: the name and email address of the Google account you sign in with (scopes openid, email, profile), and the app’s own hidden data folder in your Google Drive (scope drive.appdata). Nothing else.
  • How it is used: the name and email are shown in Settings so you know which account is connected. The Drive folder stores one file with your expenses, only to give you backup and sync across your devices.
  • How it is stored: the name and email stay on your device. The expenses file stays in your Google Drive, under your control.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, sold to or transferred to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not read by people.

Pennylog’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What data the app handles

  • Expenses, categories, notes and budgets that you enter. They are stored on your device (in the browser) and in your Google Drive.
  • The name and email of your Google account, read when you sign in, only to show which account you are connected with. They stay on your device.
  • Receipt photos: reading happens on your phone. Images are neither uploaded nor stored; only the amount you confirm is kept.
  • Preferences such as language and currency, stored on your device.

What we do not do

  • No server of ours receives your data.
  • The app contains no advertising, no tracking and no usage analytics. The website uses Google Analytics only with your consent.
  • We do not sell or share data with third parties.
  • The only external service involved is Google (sign-in and Drive), under Google’s privacy policy. The first time you scan a receipt, the reading engine is downloaded from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr).
  • The website and the app files are served by our web hosting provider, which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses.

Website, cookies and Google Analytics

This applies to the website (landing page, privacy, terms and cookie pages), not to the app.

  • What we collect, with your consent: through Google Analytics 4, pages viewed, duration of the visit, referring page, language, browser and device type, and an approximate location. The IP address is not stored in Google Analytics.
  • Why: to understand how the website is used and to improve it, in aggregate form. We do not use it to identify you, for advertising, or to profile you.
  • Legal basis: your consent (San Marino Law no. 171 of 21 December 2018). Until you accept, no cookies are set and no measurement data is sent; the Google Analytics script file is still downloaded from Google, which receives technical data such as your IP address and browser details. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.
  • Recipients and transfers: Google, as our analytics provider, which may process data outside the European Economic Area under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.
  • Retention: cookies last up to 2 years; data in Google Analytics is kept for at most 14 months.

Full details, the list of cookies and how to manage them are in the Cookie Policy.

Contact form

  • What we collect: the name or username, the email address and the message you write in the contact form. Nothing else is asked.
  • Why: only to read your message and reply to it (feature requests, problem reports). We do not use it for newsletters, advertising or profiling.
  • Legal basis: your request and your consent, given by ticking the box in the form.
  • How it is handled: the form sends an email to informazioni@istore-lab.com; the message is not stored on the website. To limit abuse, a technical counter keeps a hashed form of your IP address for up to one hour. No cookies and no third-party tools are used.
  • Retention: the email is kept only as long as needed to answer you and follow up, then deleted.

Data controller and your rights

The controller is istore-lab.com, Serravalle, Republic of San Marino, contact informazioni@istore-lab.com. Personal data is processed in accordance with the law of the Republic of San Marino on the protection of personal data (Law no. 171 of 21 December 2018). You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction or objection to processing, and portability, and you may withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to the San Marino Data Protection Authority (Autorità Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali).

How to delete your data

  • From your phone: Settings → Sign out / switch account removes the data from the device.
  • From Drive: revoke the app’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions; in Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps you can delete the app’s hidden data.
  • History: Settings → Clear history deletes closed periods and their expenses, on every device you use.
