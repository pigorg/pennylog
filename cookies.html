Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short

The Pennylog website (landing page, privacy, terms and this page) uses Google Analytics to measure visits, only if you accept. Until you choose, no cookies are set and nothing is measured. Like any resource loaded from another server, the Google Analytics script file is still downloaded from Google, which therefore receives technical data such as your IP address and browser details. The Pennylog app does not use cookies for statistics or advertising.

Contact

Who is responsible

The website is run by istore-lab.com, which decides how the data described here is used (the “controller”). Serravalle, Republic of San Marino. Contact: informazioni@istore-lab.com.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves on your device. Similar technologies, such as the browser’s local storage, work in the same way. Some are strictly necessary; others, such as statistics, need your consent.

What Google Analytics measures

With your consent, Google Analytics 4 records which pages are viewed, how long a visit lasts, the page you came from, your language, type of browser and device, and an approximate location (country or city, derived from the IP address; the IP address itself is not stored in Google Analytics). We use these figures in aggregate form to understand what is useful on the website and to improve it. We do not use them to identify you, and we do not link them to the content of the app or to your expenses, which we cannot see.

Legal basis and your choice

Statistics cookies are set only with your consent (Law no. 171 of 21 December 2018 of the Republic of San Marino on the protection of personal data). You can:

  • Reject or accept on the banner shown on your first visit. The two options carry the same weight.
  • Change or withdraw your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing deletes the Google Analytics cookies from this website.
  • We ask again after 6 months. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a refusal.

Transfers outside the EU and retention

Google may process Analytics data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. Data in Google Analytics is kept for a limited time, at most 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically.

The Pennylog app

The app, at /app/, contains no analytics and no advertising. It uses only strictly necessary storage on your device: your language and currency, the sign-in state, and your local database. This storage is not shared with anyone and does not need consent. More in the Privacy Policy.

Your rights

You may ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction or objection to processing, and portability, and you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. Write to informazioni@istore-lab.com. You can also complain to the San Marino Data Protection Authority (Autorità Garante per la Protezione dei Dati Personali).

Changes

If we change the cookies we use, we update this page and the date above, and ask for your consent again where required.
