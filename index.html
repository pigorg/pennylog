Up and running in a minute

No sign-up form, no password to invent.

  1. Open and installOpen Pennylog in Chrome (or Safari on iPhone) and add it to your home screen. It opens full screen like any app.
  2. Sign in with GoogleChoose your account and allow one thing: a private folder in your Drive just for the app.
  3. Add your spendingType an amount or scan a receipt, pick a category, done. Your report updates right away.

Your spending is nobody’s business but yours

Most expense apps keep your data on their servers. Pennylog has no server at all.

  • Saved in your Google Drive, in a hidden folder reserved for the app. Switch phones and your data comes with you.
  • One narrow permission. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
  • No ads and no tracking in the app. Nothing is sold or shared. This website uses analytics only if you agree, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • Receipt photos stay on your phone. They are read on the device and never uploaded.
  • You are in control. Export a backup, clear your history, or revoke access at any time.

Questions

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Is Pennylog free?

Yes. There are no ads, and the app costs almost nothing to run because it has no servers and no database. A paid option for extra features may come later; the basics stay free.

Can you see my expenses?

No. Your data goes from your phone straight to your own Google Drive. It never passes through a server of ours.

Why Google Drive?

It gives you backup and sync without us holding your data, and without a new password to manage. If you lose or change your phone, sign in with the same Google account and your expenses are back.

What if I am offline?

Everything works. Changes are stored on the phone and sent to Drive when the connection returns.

How does receipt scanning work?

You take a photo, Pennylog reads the text on your device and suggests the total. You always confirm or correct the amount before saving. The photo is not stored or uploaded.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account and the data stays in sync, with the most recent change winning.

Does Pennylog use cookies or tracking?

The app has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits, but only if you accept the cookie banner, and you can change your choice from “Cookie settings” at the bottom of any page. See the Cookie Policy.

Which currencies and languages are supported?

English and Italian, with US dollar, euro, pound, Canadian and Australian dollar, Swiss franc and a few more. More are planned.

Will it be on Google Play?

That is the plan. Until then you can install it from this page in a few seconds, without any store.
